    A note on what is real here

    The regulations, the citations and the channel organisations are real and verifiable — follow any link above. The catalogue itself is prototype data: names, prices and passport fields were written for this build, and each item's channel attribution demonstrates how routing and provenance flow through the system rather than recording a completed purchase. Items attributed to Local thrift and estate sourcing are the ones House of Zera sources today.
