01 / Research

What the research found

Six lines of inquiry, each ending in a decision about what to build.

Research What it discovered HOZ takeaway
Where Is Value Lost? Social media accelerates trends and causes products to lose perceived value quickly. Value is increasingly determined by relevance.
Supply Chain / Modern Commerce Business systems cannot translate rapidly changing demand into physical production. We need infrastructure connecting demand signals to action.
Human Condition Identity, aesthetics, community, fashion, and sustainability must be considered together. Define HOZ's philosophy before defining its products.
Imperialism Linear systems extract value from labor, nature, economies, and culture. Circularity should redistribute value, not just reduce waste.
Third Place Digital commerce can imitate community without actually creating human connection. Community should exist independently of transactions.
Fashion Lens Independent brands lack manufacturing, trust, forecasting, collaboration, and responsiveness infrastructure. HOZ can build infrastructure for independent creators.
