    Why inclusivity in fashion matters

    The fashion industry produces clothing that isn’t always inclusive. We want to make sure that everyone has the ability to freely express themselves through how they dress — and that everyone has the ability to access it too. At House of Zera, we strive to make fashion more transparent and clothing more accessible to people like you.

      Accessibility settings

      Every setting here is saved to this browser and applies the moment you change it. Nothing is sent anywhere.

      Speech

      Read aloud on hover and focus Speaks whatever you point at or tab to. Also adds a Listen button to every piece.
      Voice control Answer the quiz and move around by speaking. Needs microphone permission; audio never leaves your device.

      Sound

      Sound cues A short tone when something is added, changed, or rejected — useful if you cannot see the confirmation.

      Seeing

      Theme
      Dyslexia-friendly font Switches to OpenDyslexic and opens up word spacing.
      Underline every link Do not rely on colour alone to mark a link.
      Bold focus ring Thickens the keyboard focus outline to 4 px in a high-visibility colour.
      Reading ruler A band that follows your pointer and dims everything above and below it.

      Movement

      Reduce motion Removes fades, slides and smooth scrolling. Set automatically if your system asks for it.
      Large touch targets Grows every button and control to at least 48 px, with more space between them.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a field.
